Privacy at GET-PC.

What happens when you contact us through this website.

Inquiries and training interest

We request your name, contact details, chosen course or service, and message so that we can respond. Email is optional. Please do not include passwords, payment card details or confidential documents.

Where information goes

When online submission is available, inquiries are saved in a private Google spreadsheet accessible to the business and authorised staff. The product catalogue publishes product information only. If you choose WhatsApp, the prepared message goes to WhatsApp and is sent only when you press Send.

Your choices

Contact GET-PC to request correction or deletion of your inquiry. You can also contact us directly instead of using a form. We keep inquiry information only for as long as needed to handle your request and related business records.

Other services

GitHub Pages hosts this site. Google provides the product feed, maps and inquiry storage. WhatsApp and the separate training portal operate under their own privacy terms. Visitor analytics is optional and disabled until configured. Do not include personal information in page links.
