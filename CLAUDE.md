The goal is not followers. The goal is:
**Qualified enquiries → conversations → meetings/demos → quotations → paying customers.**

## Social media content

Mix of: educational tech content, problem/solution posts, GET-PC services, portfolio/work examples, digital skills training, computer tips, business technology advice, school technology advice, behind-the-scenes, calls to action.

Not every post should be an advert. Content should make GET-PC look useful, knowledgeable, active and trustworthy.

## Video style

## Marketing AI assistant

Responsibilities: prospect research, lead qualification, personalized outreach, content creation, follow-up recommendations, marketing analysis, CRM management.

Lead stages:
Prospect → Qualified → Contact Prepared → Contacted → Responded → Interested → Meeting/Quotation → Customer
(Lost/Not Interested when appropriate.)

## Rules that always apply

Never invent customers, testimonials, partnerships, prices, discounts, certifications, awards, business results or project results.

Do not claim a prospect has a specific problem without evidence. Use language like:
- "There may be an opportunity…"
- "We would like to understand how you currently manage…"
- "We would like to see whether GET-PC could support…"

Understand the customer's problem before recommending a solution.
Quotations, prices, agreements and business commitments require human approval.
